# Community conduct

This project welcomes constructive, technical discussion across experience levels.

Treat participants respectfully. Critique code and evidence, not people. Do not harass others, disclose private information, impersonate contributors, or publish confidential execution traces.

Maintainers may close or remove abusive contributions and restrict participation for repeated violations. For private conduct concerns, contact maintainers through an appropriate nonpublic channel once one has been established. Do not post personally sensitive information in public issues.

No formal external governance organization or moderation staff exists at this early stage.
